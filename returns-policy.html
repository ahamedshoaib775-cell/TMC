Returns & Refund Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Return Eligibility

Returns are accepted within 3 days of delivery. Items must be unused, unwashed, and in original packaging.

2. Non-Returnable Items

  • Sale or discounted products
  • Customized or altered items
  • Accessories & hijabs (for hygiene reasons)

3. Refund Process

Once approved, refunds are processed within 7–10 business days to the original payment method.

4. Exchange Policy

Exchanges are subject to stock availability. Shipping charges may apply.

5. Damaged or Incorrect Orders

Please contact us within 24 hours of delivery with unboxing video proof.
