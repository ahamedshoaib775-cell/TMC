Shipping Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Shipping Coverage

TMC – The Modest Company currently ships across India. International shipping will be introduced soon. Please ensure your delivery address is complete and accurate.

2. Processing Time

All orders are processed within 2–4 business days after order confirmation. Orders placed on weekends or holidays will be processed on the next working day.

3. Delivery Time

Estimated delivery timelines:

  • Metro cities: 3–6 business days
  • Non-metro cities: 5–9 business days
  • Remote areas: Up to 10 business days

4. Shipping Charges

Shipping charges are calculated at checkout based on your location and order value. Free shipping may be offered during promotions or on selected orders.

5. Order Tracking

Once your order is shipped, you will receive a tracking link via SMS or email to monitor your shipment status.

6. Delays & Exceptions

Delivery delays may occur due to unforeseen circumstances such as weather conditions, courier delays, public holidays, or high-volume sale periods. TMC is not responsible for delays caused by courier partners.

7. Undelivered / Returned Orders

If an order is returned due to incorrect address, failed delivery attempts, or refusal to accept the parcel, re-shipping charges may apply.

8. Contact Us

For any shipping-related queries, please contact us at themodestcompany@gmail.com or DM us on Instagram @the.modestcompany.
